### A First Awakening In A Cold Alleyway

The story opens with Sunny, an impoverished orphan living in the outer slums of a ruined futuristic city, who gets infected by the Nightmare Spell. Instead of receiving a cozy tutorial, he is immediately dragged into his First Nightmare—a brutal survival test set inside a freezing mountain shrine guarded by a terrifying creature.

Sunny manages to survive through pure desperation, sharp instincts, and a complete lack of shame, only to wake up with an Inherent Aspect that gives him incredible power accompanied by a devastating personal flaw.

Sunny is technically in charge of his own shadows, but his True Name leaves him permanently vulnerable to becoming someone else's thrall.

---

### Plot, Navigating Dangerous Nightmares And Ancient Ruins

The narrative follows Sunny's struggle to grow stronger while fiercely guarding his secret identity and autonomy across the Forgotten Shore and beyond:

- surviving in a sunless nightmare landscape where the sea turns into black, flesh-eating liquid every night
- managing a unique Shadow Core that grows stronger by harvesting soul fragments from nightmare creatures
- navigating complex interpersonal dynamics with elite legacy sleepers who have no idea how brutal slum life actually is
- attempting to keep his Flaw—an inability to utter a single lie when asked a direct question—from ruining his life

At several points, Sunny crafts brilliant multi-layered schemes, only for his mandatory truthfulness to force him into absurdly awkward conversations.

---

### Participants In The Situation

The cast consists of young Sleepers attempting to survive impossible odds while carrying heavy personal burdens.

- **Sunny (Sunless)** – A pragmatic, cynical protagonist who values survival and personal freedom above all else, while secretly hiding a surprisingly loyal heart.
- **Nephis (Changing Star)** – A fierce, uncompromising legacy heiress driven by an absolute determination to destroy the Nightmare Spell, no matter the personal cost.
- **Cassie** – A blind sacred seer whose prophetic visions bring immense strategic value alongside terrible emotional weight.
- **Effie** – An exceptionally strong, perpetually hungry huntress who takes great delight in teasing Sunny about his height and posture.

Their bond develops mostly while roasting monster meat over small campfires in ancient, decaying ruins.

---

### Topics The Book Mentions At Tremendous Depth

The novel constructs a grim, intricate world filled with deep lore and high-stakes combat:

- the terrible price of absolute power and the true meaning of freedom versus servitude
- how ancient civilizations fell to corruption and the lingering ghosts of lost gods
- the strategic art of lying by telling partial truths due to a magical curse
- why you should never trust a friendly face in a realm where nightmares wear human skin

A marginal note advises that if a shadow starts moving independently of the light source, you should probably stop talking and prepare for battle.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "I am a shadow... and shadows cannot be killed."

This moment captures Sunny’s shift from a desperate survivor in the slums to a formidable force capable of defying impossible odds.

---

### Final Thoughts

The story delivers an addicting progression fantasy experience with exceptional world-building, clever tactical battles, and a deeply compelling protagonist.

Readers may enjoy this book if they appreciate:

- dark, high-stakes progression fantasy withLitRPG elements
- smart, resourceful protagonists who win through cunning rather than raw force
- intricate world lore and cosmic horror atmospheres
- dynamic, slow-burn rivalries and alliances

Overall rating: **10 out of 10 mischievous shadows**.
