### A Transmigration That Starts With A Gunshot

The story opens with Zhou Mingrui waking up in an unfamiliar room, wearing a Victorian suit, and realizing he has transmigrated into the body of Klein Moretti—a recent history graduate who appears to have just survived a mysterious gunshot wound to the head.

While trying to figure out how to return home, Klein accidentally conducts a bizarre ritual that connects him to a foggy realm above the gray fog. Impersonating a powerful ancient deity named "The Fool," he accidentally forms a secret society of powerful individuals who trade high-level intelligence and dangerous secrets.

It is entirely unclear who is manipulating whom, but Klein is determined to act like he planned everything from the beginning.

---

### Plot, Escalate Through Sequences And Rituals

The narrative follows Klein’s climb up the supernatural "Potion Sequences" in a world filled with eldritch horrors, secret churches, and industrial Victorian smog. His journey involves:

- sipping dangerous potions made from monster parts to gain supernatural abilities
- acting out specific roles matching his potion title to avoid going insane from corruption
- managing a secret gathering of elites while secretly being a broke history graduate
- dodging monstrous entities, cursed artifacts, and corrupting outer deities

At several points, Klein attempts to take a simple nap or write a history paper, only for a centuries-old conspiracy to land on his doorstep.

---

### Participants In The Situation

The cast operates in a world where gaining power brings you dangerously closer to madness.

- **Klein Moretti (The Fool)** – A cautious, practical protagonist who balances high-level cosmic poker games with budgeting for his next meal.
- **Audrey Hall (Justice)** – An optimistic noblewoman who joins the Tarot Club as a novice spectator and rapidly grows into a formidable mentalist.
- **Alger Wilson (The Hanged Man)** – A cunning pirate captain and Church official who overthinks every single word "The Fool" says.
- **Amon** – A monocle-wearing, terrifying villain who turns paranoia into an art form.

Their character development occurs mostly during ritualistic meetings above the gray fog and life-or-death battles in shadowy alleyways.

---

### Topics The Book Mentions At Length

The novel constructs a massive, complex magic system intertwined with cosmic horror:

- the delicate balance between gaining power and preserving human sanity
- how history, mythology, and religion are shaped by ancient, hidden powers
- the psychological toll of keeping multiple identities secret from everyone around you
- why you should never look directly at a deity or put on a monocle found on the street

A marginal note warns that if you hear whispers in the dark or see crimson moonlight, you should probably stop reading and report to the local Church immediately.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "We are guardians, but also a bunch of miserable wretches that are constantly fighting against danger and madness."

This quote summarizes the dark, uncompromising reality faced by every Beyonder who tries to protect humanity from the horrors of the cosmos.

---

### Final Thoughts

The story delivers an extraordinary world-building experience filled with incredible twists, complex magic mechanics, and unforgettable mysteries.

Readers may enjoy this book if they appreciate:

- detailed, rule-based magic systems with deep lore
- Victorian steampunk settings mixed with Lovecraftian cosmic horror
- clever, tactical protagonists who win through intelligence and preparation
- grand, sprawling web novel epics with hundreds of interconnected plot threads

Overall rating: **10 out of 10 monocle-wearing avatars**.
